Ibyerekeye AMATEGEKO Y'UMUHANDA

Urubuga **AMATEGEKO Y'UMUHANDA** rwashyiriweho gufasha Abanyarwanda bose n'abashaka gukorera uruhushya rwo gutwara ibinyabiziga (Provisional Driving License) kwiga neza no gusobanukirwa amategeko y'umuhanda n'umutekano wo mu muhanda.

Inkomoko y'Amategeko Twifashisha

Ibibazo n'amasomo yose biri kuri uru rubuga bishingiye ku mategeko yemewe n'amategeko akurikizwa muri Repubulika y'u Rwanda, by'umwihariko:

  • Iteka rya Perezida N° 85/01 ryo ku wa 02/09/2002: Rigenga amategeko rusange y'umuhanda no kugenda mu muhanda.
  • Amategeko na politiki bya Polisi y'u Rwanda (RNP): Byerekeranye n'ibizamini by'uruhushya rw'agateganyo bitangirwa kuri Irembo.
  • Ibyemezo mpuzamahanga bya Genève na Vienne: Byerekeye ibyapa by'umuhanda n'ibimenyetso mpuzamahanga u Rwanda rwashyizeho umukono.
